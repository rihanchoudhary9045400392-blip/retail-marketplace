Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RETAIL (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you browse our marketplace, create or use an account, add items to your cart, or place an order.

Please provide only accurate information needed to process your order. Do not send payment passwords, OTPs, or other confidential credentials through chat or WhatsApp.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may process information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, account details, order items, order status, and messages you send about an order. We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as browser/device details and error logs.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and keep the marketplace working.
  • To show products, maintain carts, process orders and coordinate Cash on Delivery.
  • To contact you about order confirmation, delivery, support, returns or relevant service updates.
  • To protect the service, investigate misuse, fix errors and meet applicable legal requirements.

3. Orders and WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us or submit an order through WhatsApp, information you send—including your phone number, address and order details—will be handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review WhatsApp’s policy before using that service. Do not assume that sending a WhatsApp message alone confirms an order unless we confirm it.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, such as hosting, database/authentication providers, and messaging services, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. These providers may handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Cookies, storage and security

The website and its technical services may use cookies or browser storage to maintain sessions, remember preferences or support basic functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not work properly if they are blocked. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, handle orders and disputes, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete personal information, or raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Children

RETAIL is intended for people who can lawfully make purchases. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

8. Third-party services and links

Some functions may rely on third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one. We encourage you to review those policies before sharing information with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website or its features change. The latest version and its “Last updated” date will be published on this page.

10. Contact about privacy

For a privacy question or a request to correct or delete your information, use the contact or order-support method made available on the RETAIL website. Please do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information in your message.
